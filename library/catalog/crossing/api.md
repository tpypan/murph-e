`ARCADE.crossing(config)` returns `{init(api),update(api,dt),draw(api),inspect(),layout()}`. Create inside top-level init and delegate update/draw. Complete original frog, vehicle, log and turtle animations are bundled; do not regenerate their pixels. `api.players` chooses solo or same-board 2P cooperation, never versus.

Supported options: `levels` integer1–8 default3; `lives`1–9 default3 per player; `timeLimit`10–180 seconds default90 per level; `laneSpeed`0.25–2 default1; `turtles:false` replaces turtle groups with always-floating logs (defaulttrue); `startColumns:[6,8]` two starting grid columns0–13; `sound:false` disables event sounds. `onEvent(event,api)` receives detached death/home/level events. `drawOverlay(api,{level,filled,timeLeft,phase,layout})` retains the existing hook and adds detached geometry. `inspect()` is a detached diagnostic snapshot, not a mutator. No arbitrary-character or arbitrary-lane config exists; new requested identities/geometry need explicit implementation, not invented keys.

For a home notice use `homeNotice:'HOME'`, with optional `homeNoticeSeconds` (default1, bounded0.2–3). It appears only after an actual home, in that player's color and separate bottom HUD slot, and does not change scoring. Text is uppercase printable ASCII, capped at7 characters. Notices are disabled by default. This option owns its timing and reset; do not add another HOME overlay callback.

`layout()` after init returns fresh `{scoreHud,status,field,timer,notices}` rectangles `{x,y,w,h}`; notice rectangles also have `player`. The field occupies(16,28,224,176), the protected TIME area(80,211,64,8). P1's notice slot is(16,211,56,8), P2's(168,211,56,8). Use only those notice rectangles for custom small text; never center an overlay at y211 or paint over the lanes, frog positions, lives, home bays or runtime score strip. The legacy `drawOverlay` remains unrestricted for compatibility, so it can still obscure gameplay if misused. Neither layout nor inspect is a state mutation API.

Controls: directions make eight-frame buffered hops; holding repeats. A and B are unused. No second start gate. Landing on logs/turtles carries the frog by that lane's velocity. Turtles visibly warn for40frames before50frames underwater. Missing a home bay, traffic, unsupported water or being carried beyond the field costs a life. Home bays are reserved during the final hop; two players cannot overwrite a home or both score it. Reservation priority alternates, and a blocked final hop is safely refused. Five shared home bays clear a level. Players have independent lives/scores; an eliminated partner waits while the other may finish. All players out calls gameOver; final clear calls win for the team. A level transition preserves lives/scores and resets bays, clocks, lanes and positions. Lane speed grows13%per level, capped1.7×before laneSpeed multiplier.

Score:10per newly advanced row per crossing (no up/down farming),200plus remaining seconds per home. The original visible lane plan has four traffic rows, a grass bank, four river rows, and five homes. The runtime owns the top score strip; do not duplicate it. This is a compact original crossing game, not original Frogger ROM art or an unrestricted world engine.
